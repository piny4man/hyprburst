# Code Of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Hyprburst contributors and users are expected to keep discussion respectful, technical, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Respecting maintainers' scope and release decisions.
- Avoiding personal attacks, harassment, and discriminatory language.
- Keeping issue and pull request discussions relevant to Hyprburst.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sustained disruption, personal insults, discriminatory comments, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

The project owner may moderate, edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation from people who violate this code of conduct.

Official release, repository, crates.io, and AUR authority remain governed by [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
